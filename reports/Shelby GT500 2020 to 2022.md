# Predator V8 defines a three-year GT500 run

The 2020-2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 is a mechanically unchanged, three-model-year car built at Flat Rock, Michigan: a hand-built **5.2L "Predator" cross-plane V8 with a 2.65L Eaton Roots supercharger making 760 hp and 625 lb-ft**, driven through a **Tremec TR-9070 7-speed dual-clutch** to a 3.73:1 Torsen differential. It started at **$73,995 delivered in 2020** and ran past $100,000 with options ([Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125770_how-the-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-tops-100-000)). Year-to-year differences are colors, packages and the 2022 Brittany Blue Heritage Edition, so an app's per-year logic belongs mostly in paint, option and VIN data rather than in performance specs. The car was the quickest of its muscle-car peers at the drag strip (Ford claimed 10.7 s, and the best stock pass was 10.61 s), but the Camaro ZL1 1LE matched it on road courses for less money. On the aftermarket, a tune adds roughly 56 whp, a Whipple 3.8L blower reaches about 1,000 crank hp, and the DCT clutches become the limit. The sources leave real gaps for an app team. Ford never published production totals. Curb weight, Heritage Edition count and Handling Package contents conflict between sources. Fluid capacities and NHTSA campaign numbers were not retrieved. No openly licensed GT500-specific images or audio were found. This report flags each gap so the app does not present guesses as facts.

## One drivetrain, three years of paint and packages

The GT500 was unveiled at the **January 2019 Detroit auto show**. It was sold for **model years 2020-2022**, aimed at North America and the Middle East, and not sold in Europe ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)). The engine redlines at **7,500 rpm**, peaks at **760 hp at 7,300 rpm and 625 lb-ft at 5,000 rpm**, runs **9.5:1 compression**, and makes about **12 psi** of boost. A bar-and-plate intercooler sits above the rotors ([TREMEC](https://tremec.com/?p=407); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/06/2020-shelby-gt500-predator-engine-details-surface/amp); [Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=31080)). TREMEC rates the DCT's shifts at "under 100 milliseconds," while other press coverage says 80 ms ([TREMEC](https://tremec.com/?p=407)). The research found no reliably sourced gear ratios, so the app should leave them out rather than repeat the ratio set circulating online without a source.

Stopping comes from **Brembo 6-piston front calipers on 420 mm two-piece rotors and 4-piston rears on 370 mm rotors** ([Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1124390_2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-specs-deep-dive)). Standard tires are Michelin Pilot Sport 4S, with Pilot Sport Cup 2 on the Carbon Fiber Track Pack. The front size is 305/30R20 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)). The rear size, believed to be 315/30R20, is unverified. Dimensions are a **107.1 in wheelbase, 190.2 in length, 76.6 in width (81.9 in with mirrors) and 54.3 in height** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)). EPA fuel economy is **12 city / 18 highway / 14 combined mpg** from a tank of about 16 gal ([encycarpedia](https://www.encycarpedia.com/us/ford/20-mustang-shelby-gt500-coupe)).

**Conflict: curb weight.** A dealer training manual gives **4,225 lb**, spec databases list about **4,182 lb**, and some listings say **4,006 lb** ([Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1124390_2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-specs-deep-dive); [encycarpedia](https://www.encycarpedia.com/us/ford/20-mustang-shelby-gt500-coupe)). The 4,225 lb figure has the most authoritative origin, but the app should show a range with sources until a Ford spec sheet confirms one number. Top speed (often quoted as 180 mph), MagneRide calibration details and the full drive-mode list were not confirmed by any source.

Carscoops reports $73,465 before the $2,600 gas guzzler tax and $1,195 destination ([Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=2394782)), while $72,900 appears in Heritage Edition coverage. Neither figure is directly comparable with the 2020 delivered price. The packages matter more than the model years. The **Carbon Fiber Track Pack ($18,500; one source says $18,000)** bundles 20-in carbon fiber wheels on Cup 2 tires, a GT4-derived carbon wing, splitter wickers, a carbon instrument panel, Recaro seats, a rear-seat delete, adjustable strut-top mounts and wheel locks ([Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/357225/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-price/); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2020/09/2020-shelby-gt500-carbon-fiber-track-pack-explores-its-lofty-limits-video)). Sources disagree on whether the wing is fixed or adjustable, and Jalopnik says adjustable ([Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/the-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-will-start-at-70-30-1835940835)). The **Technology Package (~$3,000)** adds navigation, blind-spot monitoring, B&O audio and heated mirrors ([Carvana](https://www.carvana.com/vehicle/4805574)). **Conflict: Handling Package.** Most reports describe a Gurney flap plus splitter wickers, but the price appears as $1,500, $1,750 or C$1,895, and the full contents are disputed ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/2020-gt500-handling-package.130958/); [pcarmarket](https://www.pcarmarket.com/auction/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500/)). Treat Handling Package contents as unverified until an order guide is found.

The research also lacks a complete per-year color list with paint codes, stripe option prices, the Convenience Package and the Recaro price.

Shelby American built derivatives from this car. The **Signature Edition** adds about 40 hp. The **GT500KR**, revealed December 15, 2021 for Shelby's 60th anniversary, is limited to 180 units. The **GT500 Code Red** is a twin-turbo 5.2L making over 1,000 hp on 93 octane and up to 1,300 hp on E85, planned at about 10 cars a year ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)). Code Red is a Shelby American model, not a Ford one, and Shelby's own video shows one running **8.59 s at 161 mph** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajsZ3rrLxjs)). Full specs and pricing for these editions, and any S550 Super Snake, were not found.

## A 10.6-second car whose sticker became a ceiling, not a floor

Ford claimed **0-60 mph in 3.3 s and a 10.7 s quarter mile** on a prepped surface ([musclecarsandtrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-0-60-quarter-mile-times-revealed/)). Launch control is selectable from 1,200 to 3,200 rpm ([Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2019/12/bone-stock-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-runs-the-quarter-mile-in/)). In Car and Driver's testing the base car beat the CFTP car: **3.4 s / 6.9 s 0-100 / 11.3 s** against 3.4 / 7.1 / 11.4 s. The magazine blamed the CFTP's Cup 2 tires on an unprepped strip ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2021/01/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-is-quicker-without-the-carbon-fiber-track-pack)). The best stock passes were **10.61 s at 133 mph** in a private Bradenton test and **10.66 s** for a customer car at Texas Motorplex ([Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/379132/2020-shelby-gt500-quarter-mile/); [Dragzine](https://www.dragzine.com/?p=1864682)). A fake R&T test sheet circulated online, so the app's performance data should come only from attributable tests ([musclecarsandtrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/theres-a-fake-2020-ford-mustang-gt500-test-sheet-on-the-internet/)). Lap times are thin. The only figures found are user-submitted LapMeta times of **VIR Grand West 2:44.5 and Laguna Seca 1:33.8** in the stock class ([LapMeta](https://www.lapmeta.com/en/vi/6210)).

The market has gone through a full cycle. One early dealer asked **$185,890 for a CFTP car with a $105,890 MSRP** ([Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=1986503)), and Hagerty put the 2020 car at the top of its Hot List of future collectibles ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2020/07/2020-mustang-shelby-gt500-dubbed-future-collectible-by-hagerty/)). Barrett-Jackson produced outliers of **$154,000** (2020 Iconic Silver CFTP with stripes) and **$165,000** (2021 Oxford White CFTP) ([Ford-Trucks.com](https://www.ford-trucks.com/?p=119208)). Online auctions tell a calmer story. On PCARMarket, a **2022 CFTP with 18 miles sold for $112,000**, a 2022 Dark Matter Gray CFTP reached $94,250, a **2022 Heritage CFTP stalled at an $84,000 high bid (Aug 2024)**, and a 2021 Carbonized Gray car with about 2,450 miles went for $79,250 ([PCAR](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500); [PCAR Heritage](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-carbon-fiber-track-pack); [PCAR 2021](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2021-ford-mustang-shelby-cobra-gt500-coupe)). Hagerty-logged Mecum results range from **$118,250 sold (Dallas, Sep 2022) to a $70,000 no-sale (Schaumburg, Oct 2022)** ([Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/valuation-tools/past-sales/2020-ford-mustang/vin/1c1ff011-1ef7-4975-9379-bfccc3e54002)). The mapping of individual results to Hagerty pages was not verified. Low-mile CFTP cars now trade near or below their original sticker. The Heritage Edition's rarity has not yet earned it a premium. No 2026-dated sales and no current Hagerty condition values were retrieved.

Ford never published production figures. The documented numbers are a planned **~5,000 cars for 2020** from an internal slide ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/07/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-limited-to-5000-units/)), a **dealer claim of 5,860 built for 2020, with Grabber Lime rarest at 376** ([Legendary Motorcar](https://legendarymotorcar.com/inventory/2020-shelby-gt500-2955.aspx)), a forum estimate of about 5,800 ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/2022-gt500-production-numbers.173266/)), and a secondary estimate of **~9,000 cars across 2020-22, about 1,000 of them CFTP**, with an unclear method ([Automotive Addicts](https://www.automotiveaddicts.com/89664/how-many-shelby-gt500-cars-were-manufactured-over-the-years)). Ford told Car and Driver it does not track builds by package, and noted that the $10,000 painted stripes were surprisingly popular ([AOL/Car and Driver](https://www.aol.com/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500s-10-214500277.html)). **Conflict: Heritage Edition count.** One source says **about 225** and a Barrett-Jackson listing says **about 255** ([mustangspecs](https://www.mustangspecs.com/mustang-of-the-day-2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition/); [Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2022-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-HERITAGE-EDITION-266929)). The ownership researcher rejected a separate "225" claim from a spam-like site. The app should display the Heritage count as "approx. 225-255 (unconfirmed)." Totals for 2021 and 2022 and color splits remain unknown. The mustang6g build-statistics thread and the MustangAttitude Data Explorer pages exist but blocked fetching, so they are the next sources to try ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/build-statistics-for-2020-2022-run.179732/latest); [MustangAttitude 2022](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2022shelby.shtml)).

## Ownership records: one timing-chain action, DCT wiring, and many unknowns

The research confirmed no GT500-specific U.S. NHTSA campaign number, so the app should send users to a VIN lookup at nhtsa.gov rather than show a recall list. The issues found are as follows. **Transport Canada recall 2020-485 (Ford 20C21)** covers 833 GT350 and GT500 cars built from Sep 2016 to Jul 2020, whose body control module software can stop the daytime running lights from turning on ([Transport Canada](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/transport-canada-recall-2020485)). A **secondary timing-chain tensioner on the 5.2L may not have been activated at assembly**, with a risk of lost timing or engine failure. It was handled as an inspect-and-repair service action for 2020 GT500/GT350, with coverage through April 30, 2022, rather than as a formal recall ([The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=37802)). A TSB for 2020-21 transmission concerns and trouble codes directs technicians to check **connector C105 near the right-hand valve cover** for pushed-out terminals ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/2020-gt500-and-dtc-codes-concerns.153414/)). Owners report "powertrain malfunction / reduced power" faults, often at the track ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/powertrain-malfunction-reduced-power-anyone-else.177448/)). A vendor claims the DCT cooler lines crack or leak ([SPE listing](https://ffperformance.co/product/spe-motorsport-2020-2022-ford-mustang-gt500-dct-cooler-line-kit)), which is a vendor claim, not an independent finding. An October 2025 Mustang rear-camera recall (25S-A9) has an unconfirmed applicability to the GT500 ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/10-14-25-recall-issued.222174/post-4384572)).

The research has large maintenance gaps. The one sourced fact is **Motorcraft 5W-50 full synthetic (WSS-M2C931-E1)**, which Ford lists for the GT500 ([Motorcraft](https://www.motorcraft.com/content/dam/ford-motorcraft/es_mx/inicio/ecatalog/pdf/f-t-sae-5W-50-full-synthetic-motor-oil.pdf)). The engine is wet-sump ([Raptor forum](https://fordraptorforum.com/goto/post?id=2042984)). Oil capacity, DCT fluid type and capacity, engine and intercooler coolant, service intervals and the 2020+ break-in procedure all need the 2020-22 owner's manual and GT500 supplement. The often-quoted rule of 100 miles before extended wide-open throttle and 1,000 miles before track use comes from the 2014 supplement ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/gt500-break-in-period.127991/)). Warranty terms (presumably 3yr/36k basic and 5yr/60k powertrain) were not sourced and must be checked in Ford's Warranty Guide.

VIN decoding is straightforward. GT500 VINs read **1FA6P8SJ?[L/M/N]5xxxxxx**: 1FA is a Ford USA passenger car, **P8S is the GT500 (P8J is the GT350)**, **J is the 5.2L supercharged engine**, and **L/M/N in position 10 mark 2020/2021/2022** ([MustangAttitude](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2020mustang.shtml)). Sample VINs such as 1FA6P8SJ1L5504855 show plant code 5 and sequences starting near 500000 ([forcar](https://forcar.org/vin-check/1FA6P8SJ1L5504855/)). Plant code 5 as Flat Rock, position 4 as the restraint code and position 9 as the check digit are inferences that should be verified against NHTSA vPIC. The last digits of the VIN are stamped on the block and transmission, which helps buyers check for matching numbers ([SAAC forum](https://www.saac.com/forum/index.php?msg=90658)).

## Aftermarket power climbs to 1,000 hp, then the DCT pushes back

The usual mod path, as sourced, starts with a **Palm Beach Dyno 93-octane tune with an 8,000 rpm limiter, worth about +56 whp** ([The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=34098)). Next come pulley and fuel upgrades on E85. A 2.8 pulley car made 754 whp / 746 lb-ft on its first pull, though it is unclear whether that car was an S550 ([FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/?p=29773)). After that come blower swaps. VMP's "Apex Predator" upgrade made **908 rwhp on pump gas and 954 rwhp / 718 lb-ft on E85** at about 16 psi ([FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/news/vmp-pushes-an-apex-predator-upgraded-gt500-to-954-rwhp-with-e85/)). **Whipple's 3.8L kit (WK-2550-38)** claims up to 1,035 hp at 16 psi on 93 octane with a stock engine, costs about $8,100-8,850, and carries **CARB EO D-231-84** for MY2020-22 ([Whipple](https://whipplesuperchargers.com/i-2046); [MAK Performance](https://makperformance.com/products/ford-shelby-gt500-3-8l-supercharger-upgrade-kit-2020-2022)). Listings disagree on whether it is the Gen 5 or Gen 6 blower ([Coyote Direct](https://coyotedirect.com/products/whipple-superchargers-wk-2550-38-w235rf-gen-5-3-8l-supercharger-kit-2020-5-2l-shelby-gt500)). Forum posters put the DCT clutch rating at roughly **664-700 lb-ft**, and the idea that upgrades become mandatory above about 800 wheel lb-ft is speculation ([Mustang6G](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/dct-performance.128063/post-2650750)). No verified clutch-upgrade vendor was found.

The dated drag milestones run as follows. Bolt-on cars on the stock pulley ran **9.7-9.9 s** (SPE intake, Lethal race pipe, PBD tune, drag radials) ([HotCars](https://www.hotcars.com/stock-pulley-mustang-shelby-gt500-quarter-mile-record/)). A 2020 car ran a then-record **9.70 s at 144.57 mph** ([The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=34398)). Lethal Performance ran **8.86 s at 163 mph** with a stock-motor Whipple 3.8L car ([FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/?p=938086)). Palm Beach Dyno ran **8.80 s at 158 mph** on a stock engine and DCT, according to PBD's video ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6F8hEli7IE4)). Current (2024-26) records were not verified, so the app should timestamp every record.

**Three aftermarket notes are excluded or marked unverified.**

- **"~810-820 whp" tune-only figure: excluded.** The source notes themselves say it is unsourced, so the app should cite +~56 whp instead.
- **"Whipple 2.3L" in the PBD 8.8 s build: unverified.** The researcher's summary of The Mustang Source article ([link](https://themustangsource.com/?p=38704)) calls the blower spec suspect. A 2.3L Whipple would be smaller than the stock 2.65L unit, so the blower in that build should be listed as unknown. The 2.75-in pulley, Kelltrac rear suspension and slicks come from the same unverified summary.
- **"PBD ~1,100 rwhp / 9.035 s at ~1,141 rwhp": unverified.** The cited FordMuscle page is the same one used for the Lethal 8.86 s run, and the PBD attribution was not confirmed. The app should not publish it until the original source is found.

Under the Magnuson-Moss Act, as forum readers interpret it, Ford must show that a modification caused a failure before denying a warranty claim. The aftermarket parts themselves are never covered ([Mustang6G](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/will-a-tune-void-warranty-no.210386/)). No GT500 warranty-denial case and no CARB EOs for tunes or exhausts were found.

The car's rivals split by venue. In Car and Driver's drag test the **GT500 ran 11.4 s at 132 mph, the ZL1 1LE 11.5 s at 124 mph, and the 797 hp Hellcat Redeye 11.6 s at 128 mph** ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2020/02/2020-shelby-gt500-vs-camaro-zl1-1le-vs-challenger-hellcat-redeye-at-the-drag-strip)). Edmunds had the GT500 CFTP at 11.3 s ([Edmunds](https://edmunds.com/ford/shelby-gt500/2020/review)). On Throttle House's lap test, the **ZL1 1LE ran 1:27.20 against the GT500's 1:28.15 on worn tires** ([Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=1967930)). Car and Driver's tested prices were **$94,665 for the GT500 versus $73,995 for the Camaro** ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2020/01/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-v-2019-chevrolet-camaro-zl1-1le)). The 670 hp C8 Corvette Z06 lapped Buttonwillow 3 s quicker than a GT500 Track Pack car ([duPont Registry](https://news.dupontregistry.com/chevrolet/corvette/watch-a-c8-corvette-z06-go-head-to-head-against-a-shebly-gt500-track-pack/)). Specs for the Porsche 911 and the Challenger Jailbreak were not gathered.

Owner communities are concentrated on **Mustang6G**, Track Mustangs Online and forums.shelby.com. r/GT500 exists but skews toward 2007-14 cars ([TMO](https://trackmustangsonline.com/threads/2020-gt500-kylo.15994/); [r/GT500](https://reddit.sudovanilla.org/r/GT500)). New buyers got the **free GT500 Track Attack** at the Ford Performance Racing School in Charlotte ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/12/gt500-track-attack-is-free-to-all-2020-gt500-buyers/)). SVTPerformance, Facebook groups, SAAC and event calendars were not verified.

## Media can be embedded freely, but almost none can be owned

No openly licensed GT500-specific images, audio or 3D models were found. A Wikimedia Commons category search returned nothing ([Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Shelby+GT500+2020&ns14=1)). Ford's From the Road terms state that Ford "grants no license" to its intellectual property ([Ford terms](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/terms-and-conditions.html)). Ford Archives licenses images from **$250 per still** for publications and from $100 per image per post for social media marketing ([fordimages](https://fordimages.archivea.com/pages/copyright-licensing)). Press photos, including the Ford-credited 113-image Auto123 gallery, should be used as references only, with written permission from Ford Communications required for app use ([Auto123](https://www.auto123.com/en/multimedia/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-pictures/120317)). Code Red media should be requested from Shelby American. RenderHub sells 2020 GT500 models, but at least one is marked **Editorial Use Only** ([RenderHub](https://www.renderhub.com/djvimoo1031/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-2020)). Because "Shelby®," "GT500®" and the Cobra emblem are trademarks (Ford owns the Cobra and licenses it to Shelby), plain-text nominative use is the safe default. Logos require licences from Ford Brand Licensing and Carroll Shelby Licensing ([IP attorney summary](https://www.iptrademarkattorney.com/los-angeles-trademark-shelby-car-racing-motorsports-automobiles-lawyer/)). Those trademark sources date from 2001-2008, and the guidance is not legal advice.

## Conclusion

Because the GT500 never changed mechanically, its data model is simple. One spec sheet covers all three years, with per-year layers for paint, packages, pricing and VIN year codes. The hard part is provenance. The figures owners care most about, such as build counts, Heritage rarity, curb weight, fluid capacities and record runs, are the least officially documented and the most often repeated without sources. An app that attaches a source and confidence level to each data point, shows conflicts as ranges, and timestamps records would be more reliable than most existing GT500 sources.

The next research round should cover the 2020-22 owner's manual and GT500 supplement, a Ford order guide with color codes, NHTSA VIN-level recall data, the blocked MustangAttitude and mustang6g production pages, and licensing conversations with Ford, Carroll Shelby Licensing and video creators. The market has also turned in buyers' favor, with CFTP cars now trading at or below sticker, so live valuation tracking could be a stronger app feature than nostalgia.
