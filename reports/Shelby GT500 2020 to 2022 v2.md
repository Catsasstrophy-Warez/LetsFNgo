# Predator V8 anchors a three-year GT500 dataset

The 2020-2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 is one mechanical car across three model years: a hand-built **5.2L "Predator" cross-plane V8 with a 2.65L Eaton Roots supercharger making 760 hp at 7,300 rpm and 625 lb-ft at 5,000 rpm**, driving a **Tremec TR-9070 7-speed DCT** (gears 3.14/2.05/1.43/1.10/0.86/0.68/0.56) into a 3.73:1 Torsen differential, electronically limited to 180 mph ([TREMEC](https://tremec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Product.Sheet_TR-9070.DCT_.pdf); [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125737_first-drive-review-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-boasts-drag-car-speed-road-racer-moves)). Per-year logic in an app therefore lives in paint codes, packages, pricing, VIN year codes and recall populations, not in the spec sheet. Eight research rounds have now closed most of the first report's large gaps: a 2021 GT500 owner supplement supplies fluids, capacities and the service schedule; NHTSA Part 573 documents establish that **20V573 (brake pedal bracket) explicitly covers GT500 DCT cars** and 23V727 very likely does; most paint codes are sourced; and a 2026 sales series puts a typical 2020 car at **about $95,000 median in 2026**, up from $86,500 in 2024 ([DriveIndex](https://driveindex.com/cars/ford/mustang-shelby-gt500/2020)). Shelby American's editions are now priced (Signature Edition upgrade $29,995, GT500KR from $127,895, Code Red $209,995 plus donor). What remains genuinely unknown is still meaningful: Ford never published 2021 or 2022 production, curb weight still has three competing figures, the Heritage Edition count is 225 or 255, the Handling Package contents are unresolved, and the 2020 and 2022 owner supplements were never obtained. Confidence tags below use **high** (primary or manufacturer document), **medium** (credible secondary or consistent multiple sources) and **low** (single snippet, forum, vendor claim).

## One spec sheet, now with gear ratios and fluids

The car was publicly revealed on **January 14, 2019 at NAIAS Detroit** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-01-14/ford-debuts-most-powerful-mustang-ever-2020-ford-shelby-gt500)), built at Flat Rock, Michigan for MY2020-2022, and sold for North America and the Middle East rather than Europe ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)). The engine runs **9.5:1 compression, a 7,500 rpm redline and about 12 psi** with a bar-and-plate intercooler above the rotors ([TREMEC](https://tremec.com/?p=407); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/06/2020-shelby-gt500-predator-engine-details-surface/amp); [Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=31080)). Bore and stroke are **94 x 93 mm** (medium; secondary) ([CJ Pony](https://www.cjponyparts.com/resources/news/2020-shelby-gt500)).

The gear-ratio gap from round 1 is resolved. TREMEC's product sheet lists "Ratio A" as **3.14/2.05/1.43/1.10/0.86/0.68/0.56, reverse 2.76**, with a **900 Nm (664 lb-ft) torque capacity**, 9,000 rpm input limit, 104 kg wet weight and shifts "as fast as 80 milliseconds"; a snippet of Ford's owner manual matches 1st, 2nd, 7th and reverse (high for ratios) ([TREMEC sheet](https://tremec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Product.Sheet_TR-9070.DCT_.pdf); [Ford owner manual](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/Ford_Content/vdirsnet/OwnerManual/Home/Content?variantid=6365&languageCode=en&countryCode=USA&Uid=G2032898&ProcUid=G2032899&userMarket=USA&div=f&vFilteringEnabled=False&buildtype=web)). That 80 ms figure supersedes the "under 100 ms" interview quote, and the 664 lb-ft rating matches what forum posters cited. A "Ratio B" set (3.24 ... 0.50) circulates but is not tied to the GT500 ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/attachments/tremec_tr-9070-pdf.463654/)). At 0.56 x 3.73, 180 mph needs only about 4,900 rpm, so the limiter, not gearing, sets top speed (inference).

## Production stays partly invisible, but values have firmed

**Production (no Ford figures exist).** The best-supported 2020 number is **5,860**, from a mustang6g compilation echoed by a dealer, with colors including "Fast Pitch"/Performance Blue 843, Shadow Black 829, Rapid Red 775, Oxford White 737, **Grabber Lime 376 and Race Red 298** (medium) ([mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3548209); [Legendary Motorcar](https://legendarymotorcar.com/inventory/2020-shelby-gt500-2955)). Note the conflict: the dealer calls Grabber Lime rarest, yet Race Red's 298 is lower in the same compilation. Ford's planning slide said ~5,000 ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/07/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-limited-to-5000-units/)), and a Barrett-Jackson "one of 5,000" claim is unreliable ([Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2020-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-244661)). For the whole run, only an unexplained estimate of **~9,000 total, ~1,000 CFTP** exists (low) ([Automotive Addicts](https://www.automotiveaddicts.com/89664/how-many-shelby-gt500-cars-were-manufactured-over-the-years)); forum members report "nothing for 21" ([Mustang6G](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/build-statistics-for-2020-2022-run.179732/latest)). **Unresolved: Heritage Edition count, 225** ([mustangspecs](https://www.mustangspecs.com/for-sale-2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-7/)) **vs ~255** ([Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2022-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-HERITAGE-EDITION-266929)); mustangspecs adds that Ford never confirmed a quota.

**Market.** The 2021-22 markup era (a $185,890 ask on a $105,890 CFTP car) and Barrett-Jackson outliers of $154,000 and $165,000 have given way to a steadier market ([Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=1986503); [Ford-Trucks.com](https://www.ford-trucks.com/?p=119208)). DriveIndex does not identify venue or CFTP status, which explains much of the spread. ConceptCarz's 2025 median of $116,875 from just four sales conflicts and should be shown as such. Named results include PCARMarket's **$112,000 for an 18-mile 2022 CFTP**, a stalled **$84,000 high bid on a 2022 Heritage CFTP (Aug 2024)** and $79,250 for a 2021 Carbonized Gray car ([PCAR](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500); [PCAR Heritage](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-carbon-fiber-track-pack); [PCAR 2021](https://www-backend.pcarmarket.com/auction/2021-ford-mustang-shelby-cobra-gt500-coupe)), Mecum results from **$118,250 (Dallas, Sep 2022) to a $70,000 no-sale** ([Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/valuation-tools/past-sales/2020-ford-mustang/vin/1c1ff011-1ef7-4975-9379-bfccc3e54002)), and **Edsel Ford II's 666-mile 2020 car at $189,901 on BaT (Sep 2025)**, a provenance outlier ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2025/09/edsel-ford-iis-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-sets-new-auction-record/)). **VIN decoding.** GT500 VINs read **1FA6P8SJ?[L/M/N]5xxxxxx**: 1FA = Ford USA passenger car, P8S = GT500 (P8J = GT350), J = 5.2L supercharged, position 10 L/M/N = 2020/2021/2022 (medium) ([MustangAttitude](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2020mustang.shtml)). Sample VINs (1FA6P8SJ1L5504855, 1FA6P8SJ6M5500608, 1FA6P8SJ9M5502658) show plant 5 and sequences near 500000 ([forcar](https://forcar.org/vin-check/1FA6P8SJ1L5504855/); [forcar](https://forcar.org/vin-check/1FA6P8SJ6M5500608/); [BaT](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/2021-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-127/)). Plant 5 = Flat Rock, position 4 = restraint code and position 9 = check digit are inferences for vPIC verification. Partial VINs are stamped on block and transmission for matching-numbers checks ([SAAC forum](https://www.saac.com/forum/index.php?msg=90658)).

## Shelby American editions, rivals and the owner world

**Shelby American editions** are post-title upfits, not Ford variants. The **Signature Edition (GT500SE)** claims **800+ hp on 93 (+40)** for **$29,995** upgrade only, capped at **100 per model year** (one 2021 release says 98 for North America, low), with pulley, high-flow intercooler and heat exchanger, adjustable springs, sway bars, caster/camber plates, 20-in wheels, dry-carbon vented hood, stripes and registry entry; widebody, Whipple and Borla are options (high) ([Shelby](https://shelby.com/Vehicles/GT500-Signature-Edition); [Shelby news](https://www.shelby.com/multimedia/News/ArtMID/5059/ArticleID/76)). The **GT500KR**, revealed December 2021 for Shelby's 60th, claims **900+ hp**, starts at **$127,895 new** or **$54,995 as an upfit** of a 2020-21 car, with **225 total including 45 overseas** (medium) ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/2021/12/16/ford-shelby-mustang-gt500kr-60th-anniversary/); [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/554870/mustang-shelby-gt500kr-2020-2022/)). **Conflict:** Wikipedia says 180 units ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)); another source splits it 120 retrofits plus 60 new; round 8's 225 supersedes but stays medium. The first production KR was auctioned for charity at Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale in January 2022 ([Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2022/01/first-production-2022-ford-shelby-gt500kr-heading-to-auction)). The **Code Red** swaps the blower for twin turbos, claiming **1,000+ hp / 780 lb-ft on 93 and up to 1,300 hp / 1,000 lb-ft on E85**, with Manley internals and a widebody; **30 cars (10 per MY), $209,995 plus donor, not street legal** (medium-high) ([Shelby PR](https://shelby.com/multimedia/News/Press-Releases/shelby-american-turns-shelby-gt500-code-red-experimental-car-into-limited-production-edition-with-up-to-1300-horsepower-1); [duPont](https://news.dupontregistry.com/blogs/mustang/new-shelby-gt500-code-red-1300-hp-twin-turbo-performance); [Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/media/?p=247116)). ## Conclusion

The research arc has inverted the original risk profile. Round 1 feared the hard engineering data (ratios, fluids, intervals, recall applicability) would be the weak spot; it is now the strongest, anchored in TREMEC, NHTSA and Ford documents. The weak spots are the collector facts owners prize most: production by year and color, Heritage and KR counts, and curb weight. That argues for an app whose valuation and rarity features display ranges with sources, while its maintenance and recall features can speak with near-OEM authority. The market data also changes the product story: 2020 medians have climbed for two straight years, so live value tracking with dated, mileage-adjusted comparables is a stronger feature than a static "price guide."
