# Round 6: GT500 (2020-22) mod pricing, gains, part numbers, guides

Round 6 was a short pass. Rate limits (HTTP 429) and forum blocks (403) stopped several fetches. **Nothing here comes from memory. Unsourced values are null.**

## Price and gain pairs
- **JLT Big Air intake CAI-GT500-20:** About $399 at retailers. JLT claims +25-30 rwhp with no tune, but there is no dyno sheet. Gives roughly $13-16 per hp, from a vendor claim only (low confidence). Source: https://tamelessperformance.com/products/jlt-cold-air-intake-2020-2021-gt500-no-tuning-required
- **VMP Stage 2 Pulley Pack (2.75in):** $1,399.99 in a search snippet only. No gain figure found.
- **VMP Predator EO Drag Pack:** $2,339.99 in a snippet. No gain figure found.
- **FordMuscle bolt-on build (fetched):** Baseline 674 rwhp / 561 lb-ft. After the full combo on E85: 828 rwhp / 666 lb-ft (+154 rwhp). The combo was a ZPE 2.65 pulley, FIC 1000cc injectors, JMS FuelMAX, Ford Performance M-6766-A52 oil separator, JLT intake, Brisk RR14S plugs and a Lund tune. The article gives no prices, no per-step numbers and no dyno type. Source: https://www.fordmuscle.com/tech-stories/our-2020-mustang-gt500-has-a-bolt-on-affair
- **PBD pulley sequence:** 1038, 1046 and 1094 rwhp on 2.7, 2.5 and 2.27 pulleys. The car was heavily modified, so the gain is not from the pulley alone. Source: https://themustangsource.com/wicked-2020-shelby-gt500-produces-1094-rwhp-37683
- **VMP Odin:** This kit is for the Coyote (Mustang GT) and F-150, not the GT500. Leave it out of the GT500 data.
- **Not found this round:** Paired price and gain for Lund, PBD, Livernois or Bama tunes. Also not found: heat exchangers, intercoolers, a priced Whipple 3.0 kit (the 3.8 is already in mods.ts), flex-fuel kits, exhaust, Kooks headers and Steeda.

## Guides
- **Track alignment:** A forum user transcribed these values from the CFTP supplement (low confidence). Front camber -2.25 and caster 8.08. Rear camber -2.20.
- **Break-in:** No 2020 Ford text was found. The 2014 GT500 supplement says 100 mi before extended WOT and 1,000 mi before competition. That is a different model year, so do not present it as 2020 guidance.
