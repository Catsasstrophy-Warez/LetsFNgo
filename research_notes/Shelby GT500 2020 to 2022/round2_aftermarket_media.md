# Round 2: Aftermarket, Records, Competitors, Communities and Media (2020-2022 Shelby GT500)

Research date: 2026-10-16. This round adds to aftermarket_community.md and media_catalog.md and does not repeat what they already cover.

## Dyno-sourced gains and prices (tunes, superchargers, heat exchangers, exhausts)

### Takeaway
A tune alone adds about 56 whp (PBD). Lund-tuned E85 builds with injectors reach about 940-945 rwhp, up from about 684 rwhp stock. I found no tune-only Lund or Livernois number for the 2020+ car. Exhaust prices are about $2,600-3,570, and I found no independent dyno data for exhausts.

### Cited Findings
- Evolution Performance "Villain" GT500 (Lund Racing tune, ID1300x injectors, E85): 940-945 rwhp / 753 rwtq, versus 684 rwhp / 591 rwtq stock. It was the first 9-second 2020 GT500 — [NMRA Digital](https://www.nmradigital.com/the-story-behind-the-first-9-second-20-gt500/); [The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=34053)
- HPJ Performance twin-turbo GT500 (Lund tune, stock engine and transmission): 1,103 rwhp / 869 rwtq on MS109 race fuel — [NMRA Digital](https://www.nmradigital.com/hpj-performance-releases-final-pictures-of-its-first-mustang-shelby-gt500/)
- PBD tune only: +56 whp. PBD's own baseline pulls ranged from 634 to 684 whp, so the starting point varied — [The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=34098)
- Livernois: the only dyno results I found were for older GT500 builds (2009 "GT1000R" at 1,006 whp with a 4.0L Whipple; "GT800R" at 862 whp). Neither is the 2020+ car — [FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/?p=881918)
- Borla ATAK cat-back for 2020-22: $2,799.99 with chrome tips, $3,569.99 with carbon tips. It keeps the active valves and includes an X-pipe — [Shop Ron Ayers](https://shopronayers.com/products/borla-2020-ford-gt500-5-2l-at-3in-atak-catback-exhaust-w-chrome-tips); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2020/07/new-borla-atak-2020-shelby-gt500-exhaust-delivers-aggressive-sound-video/)
- Corsa 3.0in active cat-back for 2020-22: about $2,610. Corsa claims "nearly 20%" more flow than stock (manufacturer claim) — [MAK Performance](https://makperformance.com/products/corsa-20-22-ford-mustang-gt500-3-0in-dual-rear-exit-catback-w-twin-5-0in-black-carbon-fiber-tips)
- VMP triple-pass dual-fan heat exchanger: the listings I found cover 2007-14 GT500 and S550 GT fitment, not the 2020+ car — [Steeda UK](https://www.steeda.co.uk/products/vmp-dual-fan-triple-pass-heat-exchanger-for-mustang-05-18-gt500-5-0l-gt)
- Whipple 3.8L (about $8,100-8,850) and the VMP 954 rwhp E85 result are already in aftermarket_community.md.

### Inferences
- A realistic E85 build with a tune, injectors and fuel upgrades makes about 900-950 rwhp on the stock 2.65L blower. That level of power puts the DCT clutches at risk (see the DCT section).

### Gaps
- I found no 2020+ dyno or price data for: a Lund tune-only gain, a Livernois tune, the VMP Odin 2.65 or Gen 2 TVS for the 2020+ car, PBD package prices, a Mishimoto heat exchanger, or Ford Performance and AWE exhausts. The only Gen 2 price I found ($6,300) is from a 2015 forum post about the 2011-14 GT500, so it does not apply to this car.

## DCT torque limits, clutch upgrades and TCM tuning

### Takeaway
The TR-9070 has 5 friction plates in the odd-gear pack and 6 in the even-gear pack. The torque figures I found (about 664-700 lb-ft clutch rating) come from forum posts. Dodson's plate-addition kit is listed for the C8 Corvette DCT, not the GT500.

### Cited Findings
- TR-9070 internals (odd pack 5 friction plates, even pack 6) — [The Shop Mag](https://theshopmag.com/features/behind-the-raw-power-driving-the-shelby-gt500)
- Dodson Superstock clutch upgrade: adds 2 plates to each pack, is rated for 700+ whp, requires a core exchange, and is listed only for the 2020-25 C8 Corvette and E-Ray — [Boost District](https://boostdistrict.com/products/dodson-superstock-clutch-upgrade-c8-corvette-dct)
- SPE Motorsport sells TR-9070 cooler lines and transmission parts, and calls the stock lines "extremely failure prone" — [SPE Motorsport](https://spemotorsport.com/collections/gt500-transmissions-components)
- Tremec showed an aftermarket TR-9070 at the 2025 Detroit Autorama — [Street Muscle](https://www.streetmusclemag.com/?p=2521868)
- PBD set a stock-engine, stock-DCT record of 8.8 s at 158 mph, with a Whipple supercharger and suspension upgrades — [FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/?p=7158)

### Gaps
- I could not confirm a GT500-specific clutch pack from Dodson, MTI or PBD, or any TCM tuning vendor details (prices, limits). These need direct vendor checks.

## Quickest and fastest GT500 records

### Takeaway
I found no 2025-2026 dated record. The fastest figure found is Shelby's 2024 claim for the Code Red (8.59 s at 161.81 mph), which uses a twin-turbo conversion.

### Cited Findings
- Shelby GT500 Code Red: 8.59 s at 161.81 mph, claimed by Shelby American (Sept 2024) — [Robb Report HK](https://robbreport.hk/posts/shelby-gt500-code-red-runs-quarter-mile-8-59-seconds); [Carscoops](https://carscoops.com/?p=3066728)
- Lethal Performance bolt-on, stock-engine 2020 GT500 with Whipple 3.8L: 8.86 s at 163 mph (Feb 2022) — [FordMuscle](https://www.fordmuscle.com/?p=6714); [The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=38704)
- Bone-stock 2020 GT500: 10.66 s — [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2019/12/bone-stock-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-runs-the-quarter-mile-in/)
- The TREMEC GT500 Shootout is a recurring event for these cars — [TREMEC blog](https://tremec-blog.com/?p=2264)

### Gaps
- The quickest built-engine GT500 times (possibly 7-second passes) for 2024-2026 are unverified. NMRA and TREMEC Shootout results pages need a check.

## Communities, events and Track Attack

### Takeaway
GT500 Track Attack was a free one-day Ford Performance Racing School program for 2020 GT500 buyers at Charlotte Motor Speedway and zMAX Dragway. Owners paid for travel. An optional second day in a Mustang GT was paid.

### Cited Findings
- Track Attack: classroom, the CMS road course, and the four-wide zMAX drag strip (launch control, drive modes). The school supplies GT500s. The optional second day uses Mustang GTs with Ford Performance suspension and brakes — [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/12/gt500-track-attack-is-free-to-all-2020-gt500-buyers/); [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/2019/12/12/ford-gt500-track-attack-school/)
- Shelby American owner forums (forums.shelby.com), including a 2020 GT500 thread — [Shelby forums](https://forums.shelby.com/topic/98214-2020-gt500-first-drive-review/)
- Mustang6G hosts the 86-page 2020 GT500 eSourceBook and a DCT thread — [Mustang6G](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/dct-performance.128063/)
- TREMEC GT500 Shootout owner drag event — [TREMEC blog](https://tremec-blog.com/?p=2264)

### Gaps
- The exact SVTPerformance GT500 subforum URL did not appear in search; the forum root is https://www.svtperformance.com/ (unverified). I did not confirm the Mustang6G GT500 subforum node URL, any Facebook group URLs or member counts, SAAC event details (SAAC: saac.com, unverified), or whether Track Attack still runs for 2021-22 buyers.
