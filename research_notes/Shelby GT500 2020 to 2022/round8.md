# Round 8: Shelby American editions, Ford Performance parts, events, media milestones

## 1. Shelby American editions on the 2020-2022 GT500
### Takeaway
Shelby American offered three main post-title packages: Signature Edition / GT500SE (800+ hp), GT500KR (900+ hp), and Code Red (twin-turbo, 1,000-1,300 hp, not street legal). This pass found no sourced Super Snake built on this platform.
### Cited Findings
- **GT500 Signature Edition (GT500SE)**: claims 800+ hp on 93 octane (+40 hp). Price: $29,995 for the upgrade only, which excludes the base car and shipping. Limit: 100 units per model year (2020-2022). Standard content: supercharger pulley, high-flow intercooler and heat exchanger, adjustable springs, sway bars, caster/camber plates, 20" wheels, dry carbon vented hood, striping and badging, leather seat covers, numbered plates, Shelby Foundation donation, and a Shelby registry entry. Options: Shelby by Whipple supercharger, Shelby Wide Body, Borla cat-back, harness bar with rear-seat delete. [Shelby](https://shelby.com/Vehicles/GT500-Signature-Edition) (high). Sources disagree on the cap: one 2021 Shelby release reportedly said 98 per MY in North America ([shelby.com news](https://www.shelby.com/multimedia/News/ArtMID/5059/ArticleID/76), via search snippet, low). The widebody is optional, not standard. Shelby says the package does not void the warranty (search summary, medium).
- **GT500KR (2022, 60th anniversary)**: 900+ hp on 93 octane. New-car price starts at $127,895. The upfit for an existing 2020/2021 GT500 costs $54,995. Total run is reported as 225, including 45 overseas. Production was due to start in Q1 2022. The first production car was auctioned at Barrett-Jackson in January 2022. Sources: [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/554870/mustang-shelby-gt500kr-2020-2022/), [Autoblog 2021-12-16](https://www.autoblog.com/2021/12/16/ford-shelby-mustang-gt500kr-60th-anniversary/), [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2022/01/first-production-2022-ford-shelby-gt500kr-heading-to-auction) (medium, from search summaries; Motor1 returned 502). Sources conflict on the new versus retrofit split (one says 120 retrofits plus 60 new).
- **GT500 Code Red (2022)**: twin turbos replace the supercharger. Shelby claims 1,000+ hp / 780 lb-ft on 93 octane and up to 1,300 hp / 1,000 lb-ft on E85. Hardware includes Manley pistons and rods plus a widebody. Only 30 will be built, 10 from each of MY2020, 2021 and 2022. Price: $209,995 plus the donor car (duPont Registry). Not street legal (Top Gear). Sources: [Shelby PR](https://shelby.com/multimedia/News/Press-Releases/shelby-american-turns-shelby-gt500-code-red-experimental-car-into-limited-production-edition-with-up-to-1300-horsepower-1), [Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/media/?p=247116), [duPont](https://news.dupontregistry.com/blogs/mustang/new-shelby-gt500-code-red-1300-hp-twin-turbo-performance) (medium-high).

## 3. Events and programs
- **GT500 Track Attack**: free for owners, who pay their own travel and hotel. It was the first owner program at the Ford Performance Racing School's new Charlotte Motor Speedway location, after the move from Utah Motorsports Campus. [Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/ford-offers-free-program-to-teach-you-how-to-drive-your-1840391534), [SAE 2020-03](https://www.sae.org/news/2020/03/new-western-home-for-ford-performance-racing-school) (medium). Years active and end date: null.
- **SAAC conventions**: SAAC-46 (2021) at Sonoma Raceway; SAAC-47 (2022) at Indianapolis Motor Speedway, around early June; SAAC-48 (2023) at the Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix. [Gentleman Racer](https://thegentlemanracer.com/2022/06/shelby-american-automobile-club-at-indy/), [Supercars.net](https://www.supercars.net/blog/saac-46-shelby-cars-converge-on-sears-point/) (medium).
- **Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale, January 2022**: the first production GT500KR was auctioned for charity (Ford Authority, medium).

## 4. Media milestones
- **2019-01-14**: public reveal at NAIAS Detroit (Cobo Center). [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-01-14/ford-debuts-most-powerful-mustang-ever-2020-ford-shelby-gt500) (high).
- **December 2020**: Road & Track named the GT500 its 2021 Performance Car of the Year. [Tremec reprint](https://tremec.com/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-awarded-2021-performance-car-of-the-year-by-road-track/) (high).
- **MotorTrend Best Driver's Car 2020**: the GT500 CFTP was a contender. It reportedly set the best Laguna Seca lap, beating the ZL1's 1:34.30, and Randy Pobst called it "the best-stopping car" of the class. [Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=37078) (low-medium, secondary). Final placement: null.
- **Car and Driver 10Best**: no source found (null).
