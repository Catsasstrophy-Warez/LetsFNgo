- Dark Matter Gray Metallic: order code HY — [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2022/05/2022-ford-mustang-gains-new-dark-matter-gray-metallic-color-first-look); [Steeda](https://www.steeda.com/2022-mustang-paint-colors-codes). Manufacturer code M7470 (PaintScratch snippet).

## Scheduled maintenance (resolved from 2021 GT500 owner supplement, layout-preserved text extraction)

### Takeaway
I extracted the text again with pdftotext -layout. It shows spark plugs at 100,000 mi and the fuel filter at 50,000 mi, which settles the round-4 ambiguity. Brake fluid is every 3 years. Differential fluid is every 150,000 mi, or every 500 mi of track use. Source: "2021 Mustang (CZG) GT500, Edition date 202012", hosted by a third party. I have not confirmed it for 2020 or 2022.

### Cited Findings
- Oil: the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor sets the interval. Do not exceed 1 year or 10,000 mi. Severe or track use: 3,000 mi or annually.
- Every 3 years: change the brake fluid.
- Every 20k mi: replace the cabin air filter, replace the DCT side filter and top off the fluid, and inspect the intercooler, intercooler coolant and supercharger belt.
- 30k mi: engine air filter. 40k mi: DCT fluid and side filter. The large main DCT filter needs no service.
- 50k mi: fuel filter. 100k mi: spark plugs, plus the first accessory-belt inspection. 150k mi: differential fluid. 200k mi: accessory belts.
- Coolant: first change at 10 yr/200k mi, then every 5 yr/100k mi.
- Track: change oil and filter after one track weekend or about 4 hours. Change rear axle fluid every 500 mi of track use or when the axle over-temperature warning appears. Before an event, replace brake fluid with DOT 4 LV (dry boiling point above 500°F).
- [Ford 2021 GT500 supplement PDF](https://autooutlet.cz/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/21-mustang-shelby-en.pdf)

## Stock dyno baselines

### Takeaway
Stock 2020 GT500 results range from about 664 to 723 rwhp, with roughly 684 rwhp typical. The spread comes from different dyno types and correction methods. No source clearly named a Dynojet for a 2020+ car.

### Cited Findings
- LMR: 668 whp at 7,160 rpm and 560 lb-ft at 4,130 rpm, run in 4th gear. Dyno brand not stated (search snippet; the page returned 403) — [LMR](https://lmr.com/products/how-much-power-does-a-stock-2020-gt500-make)
- Hennessey: 708 rwhp at about 7,300 rpm on its in-house dyno — [Yahoo/Autoblog](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-201200865.html)
- Evolution Performance: 684 rwhp and 591 rwtq stock — [NMRA Digital](https://www.nmradigital.com/the-story-behind-the-first-9-second-20-gt500/)
- Barrett-Jackson lot claim: 664 rwhp stock — [Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2020-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-252024)
- Whipple build baseline: 723 rwhp stock — [EngineLabs](https://www.enginelabs.com/?p=299250)
- PBD baselines ranged from 634 to 684 whp (round 2).
