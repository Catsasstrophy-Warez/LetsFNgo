# 2020-2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500: Ownership and Market Facts

Research note: mustangattitude.com (Data Explorer) and the mustang6g.com build-statistics thread blocked fetching (bot check / 403). Many figures below come from secondary press, dealer listings or forums and are flagged as such.

## MSRP history and current market values

### Takeaway
2020 base MSRP was $70,300, plus $1,095 destination and a $2,600 gas guzzler tax, for $73,995. For 2022, base rose to $73,465 with $1,195 destination. The Carbon Fiber Track Pack (CFTP) cost $18,500. Fully optioned cars ran about $97k-$106k MSRP, and early dealer markups reached about $80k over sticker. Recent online auction results for low-mile CFTP cars sit around $80k-$112k, with Barrett-Jackson outliers at $154k-$165k.

### Cited Findings
- 2020: $70,300 base, $1,095 destination and $2,600 gas guzzler tax = $73,995 — [Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/the-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-will-start-at-70-30-1835940835); [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/357225/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-price/about:blank)

## Production numbers

### Takeaway
Ford doesn't publish build totals by color or package. Ford planned about 5,000 units for 2020, and a dealer claims 5,860 were built. One secondary source estimates about 9,000 cars for 2020-22, about 1,000 of them CFTP. Year-by-year totals for 2021 and 2022 are unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Ford planned about 5,000 units for 2020 (internal slide) — [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/07/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-limited-to-5000-units/)
- A dealer claims 5,860 were built for 2020, with Grabber Lime the rarest at 376 — [Legendary Motorcar](https://legendarymotorcar.com/inventory/2020-shelby-gt500-2955.aspx) (dealer claim)
- Forum estimate of about 5,800 2020 cars; a member noted Ford hadn't released numbers — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/2022-gt500-production-numbers.173266/)
- About 9,000 units in this generation, about 1,000 of them CFTP — [Automotive Addicts](https://www.automotiveaddicts.com/89664/how-many-shelby-gt500-cars-were-manufactured-over-the-years) (secondary, unclear method)
- Ford doesn't track production by package; painted stripes ($10k) were surprisingly popular — [AOL/Car and Driver](https://www.aol.com/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500s-10-214500277.html)
- A forum build-statistics thread for the 2020-22 run exists but couldn't be fetched (403) — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/build-statistics-for-2020-2022-run.179732/latest)
- MustangAttitude Data Explorer pages exist for each year but were blocked by a bot check — [2020](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2020shelby.shtml), [2021](https://www.mustangattitude.com/mustang/2021shelby.shtml), [2022](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2022shelby.shtml)

### Gaps
- Totals for 2021 and 2022, color splits and Heritage Edition count are unverified. A "225 for 2022" claim came from a spam-like site and was rejected.
- No Marti Report data was found. Marti covers Ford production, but nothing published for these years turned up.

## Recalls, TSBs and known issues

### Takeaway
No GT500-specific U.S. NHTSA campaign number was confirmed. Documented items:
- A Canadian DRL/BCM software recall
- A secondary timing chain tensioner service action on the 5.2L
- A DCT TSB about the C105 connector
- DCT cooler-line leaks and track-only reduced-power faults (forum and vendor reports)

### Cited Findings
- Transport Canada recall 2020-485 (Ford ref 20C21) covers GT350/GT500 built Sep 2016-Jul 2020, 833 vehicles. BCM software can prevent the DRLs from turning on — [Transport Canada](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/transport-canada-recall-2020485); [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=2123677)
- 5.2L secondary timing chain tensioner possibly not activated at assembly, with risk of lost timing or engine failure. Covered 2020 GT500/GT350; inspect and repair, coverage through Apr 30, 2022. Described as a service bulletin or field action, not a formal recall — [The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=37802)
- TSB for 2020-21 GT500 transmission concerns and DTCs: inspect connector C105 near the RH valve cover for pushed-out terminals or harness damage — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/2020-gt500-and-dtc-codes-concerns.153414/)
- Owner reports of "powertrain malfunction / reduced power", loss of upshifts and "select manual mode", some only at the track — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/powertrain-malfunction-reduced-power-anyone-else.177448/)
- DCT cooler hard lines and seals reported to crack or leak — [SPE kit listing](https://ffperformance.co/product/spe-motorsport-2020-2022-ford-mustang-gt500-dct-cooler-line-kit) (vendor claim)
- October 2025 rear-view camera recall 25S-A9 on the Mustang (no remedy yet); applicability to GT500 unconfirmed — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/10-14-25-recall-issued.222174/post-4384572)

### Gaps
- NHTSA campaign numbers (e.g., any 20V/21V/22V entries) weren't retrieved. Check nhtsa.gov/recalls by VIN.
- No sourced data on intercooler pump failures, track cooling limits or carbon wheel cracking on the 2020+ car. Intercooler pump complaints found were for the S197 GT500.

## Maintenance, fluids, break-in and track use

### Takeaway
Motorcraft 5W-50 full synthetic (WSS-M2C931-E1) is Ford's listed oil for the GT500. Oil capacity, DCT fluid, coolant and the 2020+ break-in text weren't verified from the 2020+ owner's manual.

### Cited Findings
- Motorcraft SAE 5W-50 full synthetic is recommended for the Shelby GT500, and meets API SP and Ford WSS-M2C931-E1 — [Motorcraft data sheet](https://www.motorcraft.com/content/dam/ford-motorcraft/es_mx/inicio/ecatalog/pdf/f-t-sae-5W-50-full-synthetic-motor-oil.pdf)
- The Predator 5.2L in the GT500 is wet-sump — [Raptor forum](https://fordraptorforum.com/goto/post?id=2042984)
- Earlier GT500 supplement (2014), quoted by forum users: at least 100 mi before extended WOT, at least 1,000 mi before track or competition use, vary speeds — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/gt500-break-in-period.127991/). This is not confirmed for 2020+.

### Gaps
- Oil capacity for the 5.2L Predator, Tremec TR-9070 DCT fluid type and capacity, coolant type, intercooler coolant, service intervals and Ford's track-use checklist: not sourced. The 2020-22 owner's manual and GT500 supplement are needed.

## VIN decoding

### Takeaway
A 2020-22 GT500 VIN reads 1FA6P8SJ_[L/M/N]5xxxxxx:
- Positions 1-3: 1FA (Ford, USA, passenger car)
- Positions 5-7: P8S (GT500 fastback; P8J is the GT350)
- Position 8: J (5.2L supercharged V8)
- Position 10: L = 2020, M = 2021, N = 2022
- Position 11: 5 (Flat Rock plant)

### Cited Findings
- 1FA = Ford USA passenger car; P8S = GT500, P8J = GT350; engine code J = 5.2L supercharged, 760 hp / 625 lb-ft — [MustangAttitude 2020 Mustang](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2020mustang.shtml) (via search snippet)
- Example VINs show the pattern: [1FA6P8SJ1L5504855](https://forcar.org/vin-check/1FA6P8SJ1L5504855/) and [1FA6P8SJ6M5500608](https://forcar.org/vin-check/1FA6P8SJ6M5500608/). Both have the L or M year code and 5 for the plant, with sequences starting near 500000.
- Last digits of the VIN are stamped on the block and transmission — [SAAC forum](https://www.saac.com/forum/index.php?msg=90658)

### Inferences
- Position 4 (6) is the restraint system code, and position 9 is the check digit, per standard Ford/NHTSA convention. Position 11 = 5 is Flat Rock, inferred from the sample VINs. Verify these with NHTSA vPIC.

### Gaps
- No primary Ford VIN decoder document was retrieved.
