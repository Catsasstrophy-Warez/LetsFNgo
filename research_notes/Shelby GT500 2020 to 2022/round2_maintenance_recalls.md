# 2020-2022 Shelby GT500: Maintenance, Warranty, Recalls (Round 2)

Method note: The Ford owner manual and its GT500 supplement could not be retrieved (no direct PDF found). The lmr.com capacity page returned 403. Most fluid figures below come from search-result snippets of retailer and forum pages, which are secondary sources. Recall data was pulled directly from the NHTSA API on 2026-10-16.

## Fluids, capacities and specs

### Takeaway
Oil is 5W-50 full synthetic, and the most-cited capacity is 11.5 qt with a Motorcraft FL-2087 filter. That figure is unverified: one source says 10 qt with an FL2062 filter, and Ford media material shows an 11-qt pan. The DCT needs Motorcraft XT-14-L GT500 dual-clutch fluid, a part made for this transmission. A retailer list puts DCT capacity at 8.0 qt, while owners report a drain-and-fill takes about 3-4 qt. No coolant, intercooler, differential, brake fluid or spark plug data was found from a usable source.

### Cited Findings
- Oil: 11.5 qt, 5W-50 full synthetic, Motorcraft FL-2087 filter (retailer guide for 2020-22 GT500) — [LMR](https://lmr.com/products/2020-2022-shelby-gt500-oil-change-oil-life-reset)
- Conflicting oil figures:
  - An ALLDATA-sourced list (as summarized by search) gives 10 qt and an FL2062 filter for the 5.2L — [LMR capacities](https://lmr.com/products/mustang-fluid-capacity-2018-2020) (page itself 403)
  - Press reports an 11-qt figure — [NOLN](https://www.noln.net/topics/news/article/11482035/mustang-shelby-gt500-2019-08-12); [Ford GT500 Engine Infographic](https://media.ford.com/content/dam/fordmedia/North%20America/US/2019/08/05/GT500-Engine-Infographic.pdf)
  - Forum thread: "8.5 qt capacity but 7.5 qt for oil change" — [Shelby forums](https://forums.shelby.com/topic/77139-85-qtr-capacity-but-75-qtr-for-oil-change/). The title only was seen, and it's unclear which engine the thread covers.
- DCT fluid: Motorcraft XT-14-L, "specifically formulated for use in the GT500 7-Speed Tremec Dual Clutch transmission". The spec number is listed as N/A — [Motorcraft](https://www.motorcraft.com/us/en_us/home/our-products/chemicals-and-lubricants/transmission-fluid/gt500-dual-clutch-transmission-fluids.html)
- DCT capacity:
  - 8.0 qt per the ALLDATA-derived list — [LMR capacities](https://lmr.com/products/mustang-fluid-capacity-2018-2020)
  - Owners report a drain-and-fill used "the better part of 4qt" (others say about 3 qt) — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/trans-fluid-capacity-4qt.19526/latest)
- XT-11-QDC (WSS-M2C200-D2) is a dry-clutch fluid and is not the GT500 fluid. Mobil DCT fluid lists approval to WSS-M2C936-A. No source tied that spec to the GT500 — [eBay listing](https://ebay.com/itm/193445177680); [Mobil](https://www.mobil.com/en-cn/passenger-vehicle-lube/pds/ap-zh-mobil-dct-fluid)

### Inferences
- The 11.5 qt figure probably refers to a refill with the filter on the dry-sump-style 11-qt pan system. This should be confirmed against the manual.

### Gaps
- No manual-sourced data was found for:
  - Engine coolant type and capacity, and intercooler (low-temp loop) capacity
  - Rear differential fluid
  - Brake fluid spec
  - Spark plug part number and gap
  - Tire pressures
- The owner manual or GT500 supplement PDF was not located (owner.ford.com and fordservicecontent.com were not reached).

## Service intervals and break-in

### Takeaway
No official 2020+ GT500 break-in text or track-use interval was found. The only manual-quoted break-in guidance comes from a 2014 GT500 supplement, quoted on a forum: no extended wide-open throttle for the first 100 miles, no track or competition use before 1,000 miles, and vary your speed. Owners commonly change oil early, at 500-1,000 miles.

### Cited Findings
- 2014 supplement guidance, quoted by a forum user who says it is likely similar for 2020 — [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/gt500-break-in-period.127991/); [mustang6g](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/waiting-for-my-gt500-engine-break-in.138148/)
- Owner practice: oil changes at 500/1,000 or 500/2,000 miles, then every 3k miles or 6 months (anecdotal) — [Shelby forums](https://forums.shelby.com/topic/46409-first-oil-change/)
- Oil-life reset procedure exists for the 2020-22 GT500 (Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor) — [LMR](https://lmr.com/products/2020-2022-shelby-gt500-oil-change-oil-life-reset)

### Gaps
- No official Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor maximum interval (Ford typically caps it at 10,000 mi / 1 yr; unverified for GT500).
- No Ford track-use service schedule (DCT fluid, diff, brake fluid after track use) was found.

## Warranty

### Takeaway
Ford's standard terms are 3 yr/36k bumper-to-bumper and 5 yr/60k powertrain. Corrosion perforation is 5 yr/unlimited miles. Non-perforating corrosion is reported at 3 yr/36k. No GT500-specific warranty guide was found.

### Cited Findings
- 3/36 New Vehicle Limited Warranty, 5/60 powertrain, 5 yr/unlimited corrosion perforation — [AutoGuide](https://AutoGuide.com/buyers-guide/ford-warranty/); [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/reviews/26820/ford-warranty); [Chaiz (Mustang, commercial blog)](https://www.chaiz.com/blog/ford-mustang-warranty-cost)
- Non-perforating corrosion 3/36 — [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/money/warranties-guarantees/your-guide-to-car-warranties-a1088768196/)
- XT-14-L DCT fluid "meets all powertrain warranty requirements" — [Motorcraft](https://www.motorcraft.com/us/en_us/home/our-products/chemicals-and-lubricants/transmission-fluid/gt500-dual-clutch-transmission-fluids.html)

### Gaps
- No sourced figure for the emissions warranty (federal 8 yr/80k on major components is the standard; not verified here).
- No source on carbon-fiber wheel coverage or track-use exclusions.
- The official 2020-2022 Ford Warranty Guide PDF was not retrieved.

## Recalls, TSBs and complaints

### Takeaway
The NHTSA API lists Mustang-wide recalls for 2020-2022. None of the summaries mentions the GT500 or 5.2L by name, so whether each applies requires a VIN check. Two GT500-specific Ford campaigns are reported in secondary sources:
- 20C21: daytime running light BCM software, 833 GT350/GT500 cars
- A 2020 GT350/GT500 secondary timing-chain tensioner TSB, possibly tied to a campaign numbered 22E08

### Cited Findings (NHTSA campaign numbers; source: [NHTSA API 2020](https://api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/recallsByVehicle?make=ford&model=mustang&modelYear=2020), [2021](https://api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/recallsByVehicle?make=ford&model=mustang&modelYear=2021), [2022](https://api.nhtsa.gov/recalls/recallsByVehicle?make=ford&model=mustang&modelYear=2022))
- 2020:
  - 20V306000: forward camera misalignment
  - 20V573000: brake pedal bracket may fracture, automatic-transmission cars. The DCT GT500 might be included; unverified.
  - 20V263000: "Transmission Not In Park" chime too short
  - 20V575000: rearview camera connection
  - 22V382000: 5.0L manual cars only. N/A for GT500.
- 2020-2022: 23V727000, brake fluid level sensor warning does not activate (FMVSS 135)
- 2021-2022: 25V689000, remedy re-recall of 23V727
- 2021: 21V980000, side curtain air bag inflator
- 2021-2022: 22V083000, passenger knee air bag weld
- 2022:
  - 22V334000: forward camera/IPMA
  - 23V070000: automatic-transmission park bolt; Ford 23S06 per secondary source
  - 24V493000 and 25V096000: EPAS secondary torque sensor calibration, plus its re-recall
- Multi-year:
  - 25V442000: backup camera software, 2020-2022
  - 25V455000: fuel pump, 2021-2022
  - 25V695000: rear camera, listed under the 2020 Mustang
- Ford 20C21: DRL BCM software update, 833 GT350/GT500 — [Collision Repair Mag](https://www.collisionrepairmag.com/?p=43555)
- 2020 GT500/GT350 secondary timing chain tensioner not activated at assembly. A TSB required dealer inspection before delivery, with a repair window to 2022-04-30. A forum post references "Recall 22E08" — [The Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=37802); [DealerRater](https://www.dealerrater.com/recalls/Ford/Shelby-GT500)
- NHTSA complaints mentioning "GT500"/"Shelby":
  - 2020: 0 of 120 Mustang complaints
  - 2021: 1 of 55, ODI 11522730 (loss of power steering, blank cluster)
  - 2022: 0 of 50
  - Source: [NHTSA complaints API](https://api.nhtsa.gov/complaints/complaintsByVehicle?make=ford&model=mustang&modelYear=2021)

### Inferences
- Electronics and camera recalls likely apply to GT500s with those features. 22V382 does not apply.

### Gaps
- No per-recall GT500 VIN applicability was established.
- No SSM numbers were found.
- The 22E08 recall and the NHTSA number for the timing-chain campaign were not confirmed.
