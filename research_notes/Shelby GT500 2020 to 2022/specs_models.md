# 2020-2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 (S550): Specs and Model-Year Changes

Note: media.ford.com now redirects to fromtheroad.ford.com, and Car and Driver, Motor Authority and CJ Pony Parts blocked fetching. Many figures below come from search snippets of secondary sources, so verify them before the app relies on them.

## Model-year changes (2020, 2021, 2022) and special editions

### Takeaway
2020 launched the car. 2021 brought new colors and a $10,000 Carbon Fiber Handling Package. 2022 added Code Orange and the Brittany Blue Heritage Edition, and reportedly dropped the Carbon Fiber Track Pack. None of the years changed the mechanicals. Shelby American also built Signature Edition, GT500KR and Code Red cars from this car.

### Cited Findings
- Unveiled at the January 2019 Detroit auto show. Built at Flat Rock, Michigan, for model years 2020-2022. Not sold in Europe; aimed at North America and the Middle East. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)
- 2020 base price was $73,995, including destination and the gas guzzler tax. A fully loaded car came to $107,080. — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125770_how-the-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-tops-100-000); [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/370086/most-expensive-mustang-shelby-gt500/)
- 2021 colors:
  - Added: Antimatter Blue Metallic, Carbonized Gray Metallic and Grabber Yellow.
  - Removed: Grabber Lime, Kona Blue, Magnetic and Wimbledon White.
  - Fighter Jet Gray was a Mach 1 exclusive and not offered on the GT500. — [MuscleCarsAndTrucks / supercars.net summary via search](https://www.supercars.net/blog/ford-updates-the-mustang-and-shelby-gt500-for-2021-model-year)
- 2021 added the Carbon Fiber Handling Package for $10,000: 20-inch painted carbon fiber wheels, adjustable strut-top mounts, an oil catch can and wheel locks. — [MuscleCarsAndTrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/2021-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-carbon-fiber-handling-package-costs-10000/)
- 2022 changes:
  - Code Orange joined the palette.
  - The Heritage Edition marked the 1967 GT500's 55th anniversary. It was Brittany Blue with Wimbledon White stripes (vinyl or painted) and had no mechanical changes.
  - Pricing: base MSRP $72,900. The Heritage package cost $2,140 with vinyl stripes or $12,140 with hand-painted stripes. — [The Speed Journal](https://www.thespeedjournal.com/limited-edition-2022-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition/); [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1134161_2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-gets-retro-look)
- Heritage Edition production is uncertain. One source says about 225, while a Barrett-Jackson listing says about 255. — [Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2022-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-HERITAGE-EDITION-266929); [mustangspecs](https://www.mustangspecs.com/mustang-of-the-day-2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition/)
- The Carbon Fiber Track Pack was reportedly discontinued for 2022, according to a dealer listing. — [mustangspecs](https://www.mustangspecs.com/for-sale-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-carbon-fiber-track-pack-2/)
- Shelby American editions: — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)
  - Signature Edition: about +40 hp over the stock GT500.
  - GT500KR: revealed December 15, 2021, for Shelby's 60th anniversary; limited to 180 units.
  - GT500 Code Red: twin-turbo 5.2L making more than 1,000 hp on 93 octane and up to 1,300 hp on E85; about 10 per year planned.

### Inferences
- The 2022 base price ($72,900) appears lower than the 2020 figure ($73,995) only because the 2020 figure included destination and gas guzzler tax.

### Gaps
- Not found: an official order guide, Super Snake on the S550 GT500, full specs and prices for the Shelby American editions, and a "Code Red concept" from Ford itself (Code Red is a Shelby American model).

## Engine: 5.2L Predator

### Takeaway
Hand-built 5.2L cross-plane V8 with a 2.65L Eaton TVS R2650 Roots supercharger. Makes 760 hp at 7,300 rpm and 625 lb-ft at 5,000 rpm, with 9.5:1 compression, a 7,500 rpm redline and about 12 psi of boost.

### Cited Findings
- Output: 760 hp at 7,300 rpm and 625 lb-ft at 5,000 rpm. — [CarAdvice/spec listings via search](https://www.caradvice.com.au/781402/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-details/)
- Compression 9.5:1, redline 7,500 rpm, maximum boost about 12 psi, Eaton 2650 supercharger, 125 bar firing pressure. — [TREMEC interview](https://tremec.com/?p=407); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2019/06/2020-shelby-gt500-predator-engine-details-surface/amp)
- A bar-plate intercooler sits above the supercharger rotors, and an aluminum viscous crank damper is fitted. — [Mustang Source](https://themustangsource.com/?p=31080); [TREMEC](https://tremec.com/?p=407)
- The supercharger is a 2.65-liter Roots type, and the engine is hand-built with a cross-plane crank. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)

### Gaps
- Bore and stroke, radiator and cooler counts, and the 6-quart oil/coolant details were not verified, because the CJ Pony Predator page was blocked.

## Drivetrain: Tremec TR-9070

### Takeaway
Tremec TR-9070 7-speed DCT with shifts under 100 ms (one source says as quick as 80 ms) and a 3.73:1 Torsen limited-slip differential.

### Cited Findings
- Gearbox and differential: TR-9070 7-speed DCT co-developed with Tremec, 3.73:1 Torsen LSD. — [whichcar](https://whichcar.com.au/news/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-performance-figures); [CarAdvice](https://www.caradvice.com.au/781402/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-details/)
- Shift times: "under 100 milliseconds" per TREMEC; 80 ms per other press coverage. — [TREMEC](https://tremec.com/?p=407)

### Gaps
- Individual gear ratios were not found from any reliable source. Do not use the ratio set that has circulated without a source.

## Chassis, brakes, wheels and tires, dimensions, weight, fuel

### Cited Findings
- Brakes: Brembo front 6-piston calipers on 420 mm (16.5 in) two-piece rotors; rear 4-piston calipers on 370 mm (14.5 in) rotors. — [Motor Authority deep dive (search snippet)](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1124390_2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-specs-deep-dive); [motori.quotidiano](https://motori.quotidiano.net/news/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-numeri-dello-straripante-v8.htm)
- Tires: Michelin Pilot Sport 4S as standard; Pilot Sport Cup 2 with the CFTP. Front size 305/30R20 (rear not verified). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang); [CJ Pony (snippet)](https://www.cjponyparts.com/resources/news/2020-shelby-gt500)
- Dimensions: wheelbase 107.1 in, length 190.2 in, width 76.6 in (81.9 in with mirrors), height 54.3 in. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Mustang)
- Curb weight: 4,225 lb per a dealer training manual; other databases list about 4,182 lb, and some listings say 4,006 lb (conflicting). — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1124390_2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-specs-deep-dive); [encycarpedia](https://www.encycarpedia.com/us/ford/20-mustang-shelby-gt500-coupe)
- Fuel: EPA 12 city / 18 highway / 14 combined mpg; tank about 16 gal. — [encycarpedia](https://www.encycarpedia.com/us/ford/20-mustang-shelby-gt500-coupe)

### Gaps
- MagneRide details, spring rates and the rear tire size (believed to be 315/30R20) were not verified.

## Drive modes, tech and performance data

### Cited Findings
- Ford's claims: 0-60 mph in 3.3 s and a 10.7 s quarter mile on a prepped surface. — [musclecarsandtrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-0-60-quarter-mile-times-revealed/)
- Car and Driver tests:
  - Base car: 0-60 in 3.4 s, 0-100 in 6.9 s, quarter mile in 11.3 s.
  - CFTP car: 0-60 in 3.4 s, 0-100 in 7.1 s, quarter mile in 11.4 s.
  - The difference is attributed to the Cup 2 tires. — [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2021/01/ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-is-quicker-without-the-carbon-fiber-track-pack)
- A fake R&T test sheet circulated online. — [musclecarsandtrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/theres-a-fake-2020-ford-mustang-gt500-test-sheet-on-the-internet/)
- A dealer listing says launch control and line lock are standard. The cluster has a 220 mph speedometer. — [search snippets of dealer listings](https://www.vanguardmotorsales.com/inventory/5310/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500)

### Gaps
- The 180 mph top speed was not confirmed by any source found.
- The full drive-mode list was not found.
- Lap times are missing: MotorTrend's Laguna Seca time with Randy Pobst, VIR and Nürburgring.
