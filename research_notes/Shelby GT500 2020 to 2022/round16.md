# Round 16: fluids, tires, brakes, track noise, performance
Date: 2026-10-17. Machine-readable data is in round16.json. Confidence: high = confirmed, medium = reported, low = unverified. All prices were seen 2026-10-17.

## Found
- **Engine oil capacity** (low): 11.5 qt, which secondary parts and forum sources attribute to the 2020 GT500 supplement. Seen only in a search summary. https://lmr.com/products/2020-2022-shelby-gt500-oil-change-oil-life-reset
  - **Conflict:** NOLN (Aug 12, 2019, page read) quotes Motor Trend saying the pan holds an "almost dry-sump-like 11 quarts". That describes the pan's volume, not the service fill. https://www.noln.net/topics/news/article/11482035/mustang-shelby-gt500-2019-08-12
  - The 10 vs 11.5 qt question is still open.
- **DCT fluid** (medium): Motorcraft XT-14-L, sold as 1 qt bottles in cases of 12. It is for the GT500 7-speed TREMEC DCT only. https://www.motorcraft.com/us/en_us/home/our-products/chemicals-and-lubricants/transmission-fluid/gt500-dual-clutch-transmission-fluids.html
  - Price (low): $117.59 at IBSPOT; the pack size is unclear. https://ibspot.com/products/motorcraft-xt14l-auto-trans-fluid-shelby-gt500-dual-clutch
- **OEM tire sizes** (medium), from Michelin fitment pages for 2020–2022: front 305/30ZR20 XL 103Y, rear 315/30ZR20 XL 104Y.
  - The CFTP listing has the same sizes. https://www.michelinman.com/auto/browse-tires/by-vehicle/2022/ford/mustang/shelby-gt500/305---30ZR20---XL-103(Y)_315---30ZR20---XL-104(Y)
  - The Cup 2 index for the GT500 is not confirmed. The Cup 2 R lists 103Y/104Y.
- **Cup 2 305/30ZR20** (low): US$916 sale / $1,022 retail at Lacroix Tuning (Canada). Other variants listed at $973 and, for the N1, $904. https://www.lacroixtuning.com/305-30zr20-xl-pilot-sport-cup-2-19451-mic
- **Front pads** (low), aftermarket:
  - Hawk HB928B.644 HPS 5.0: $251.90–$294.29 (MSRP $310.99). https://fiperformance.com/products/hawk-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-hps-5-0-front-brake-pads
  - Hawk DTC-60 (HB928G.644): about $512–$717.
  - Hawk DTC-70 (HB928U.644): $544.99. https://attackingtheclockracing.com/products/hawk-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-dtc-70-race-front-brake-pads
- **Laguna Seca noise** (medium): 92 dB is the typical limit under the Monterey County ordinance. Some days are 90 dB, and there are rare 103 dB days.
  - 92 dB: https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/11-7-24-laguna-seca-driving-event-92db-weathertech-raceway-hooked-on-000409
  - 90 dB: https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/exclusive-track-day-laguna-seca-oct-14th-359-mazda-raceway-days-430207
  - 103 dB: https://motorsportreg.com/events/exclusive-laguna-seca-rare-103db-may-27th-429-weathertech-raceway-track-days-746629
- **2022 "Ford" figures** (low): MustangSpecs gives 3.4 s / 6.9 s / 11.3 s @ 132 mph. These match the Car and Driver numbers already in performance.ts, so they are probably misattributed. Not added. https://www.mustangspecs.com/?p=58615
