# Shelby GT500 2020-2022: Round 2 (Specs, Colors, Production, Lap Times)

Research note: mustangattitude.com (bot check), the mustang6g.com build-statistics thread (403) and Ford Authority's article pages (403) all blocked fetching. Several facts below therefore come from search snippets and are marked as such.

## Drivetrain gearing, top speed, wheels and tires, weight

### Takeaway
TREMEC's own product sheet gives the TR-9070 "Ratio A" ladder as 3.14 / 2.05 / 1.43 / 1.10 / 0.86 / 0.68 / 0.56, with reverse 2.76. A search snippet of the Ford owner manual reportedly shows the same 1st, 2nd, 7th and reverse values. Final drive is 3.73:1. Top speed is electronically limited to 180 mph. Wheels are 20x11 front and 20x11.5 rear, with tires 305/30R20 front and 315/30R20 rear.

### Cited Findings
- TR-9070 Ratio A, read directly from the PDF text:
  - Gears 1-7: 3.14, 2.05, 1.43, 1.10, 0.86, 0.68, 0.56. Reverse: 2.76. Ratio span: 5.6.
  - Torque capacity 900 Nm (664 lb-ft); maximum input speed 9,000 rpm; wet weight 104 kg (229 lb); shifts "as fast as 80 milliseconds."
  - The sheet says "alternative ratios available upon request."
  — [TREMEC product sheet PDF](https://tremec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Product.Sheet_TR-9070.DCT_.pdf)
- The Ford owner manual reportedly lists 1st 3.14, 2nd 2.05, 7th 0.56 and reverse 2.76, which matches Ratio A. Source is a search snippet only. — [Ford owner manual (fordservicecontent)](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/Ford_Content/vdirsnet/OwnerManual/Home/Content?variantid=6365&languageCode=en&countryCode=USA&Uid=G2032898&ProcUid=G2032899&userMarket=USA&div=f&vFilteringEnabled=False&buildtype=web)
- A forum copy of the TREMEC sheet shows a second "Ratio B" set (3.24 / 2.02 / … / 0.50, reverse 2.93, span 6.5). It is not tied to the GT500. — [mustang6g attachment](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/attachments/tremec_tr-9070-pdf.463654/)
- Final drive: 3.73:1 Torsen limited-slip, carried over from round 1. — [whichcar](https://whichcar.com.au/news/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-performance-figures)
- Top speed: electronically limited to 180 mph. — [Motor Authority first drive](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125737_first-drive-review-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-boasts-drag-car-speed-road-racer-moves)
- Wheels and tires, from a dealer listing of a CFTP car:
  - Carbon wheels: 20x11 front, 20x11.5 rear.
  - Tires: 305/30R20 front, 315/30R20 rear (Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2).
  — [PistonHeads listing](https://www.pistonheads.com/buy/listing/10496139)
- Base aluminum wheel weights, as weighed by a forum user: about 35 lb rear and 36 lb front. — [trackmustangsonline](https://trackmustangsonline.com/threads/gt500-base-oem-wheel-weights.15701/post-234623)
- Curb weight conflicts:
  - 4,225 lb per a leaked dealer training manual. — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1124390_2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-specs-deep-dive)
  - 4,006 lb as measured by Steeda with less than a gallon of fuel. — [PistonHeads](https://www.pistonheads.com/buy/listing/10496139)

### Inferences
- Because the owner manual snippet matches TREMEC's Ratio A, Ratio A is very likely the GT500's production gearing.
- In 7th gear (0.56 × 3.73 = 2.09 overall), 180 mph would need only about 4,900 rpm. That means the 180 mph figure is set by the electronic limiter, not by gearing.

### Gaps
- No Ford press kit figure for curb weight was found, so curb weight is still unresolved.
- The rear wheel width of the base (non-carbon) wheel was not confirmed separately from the carbon wheel.
- MagneRide calibration details and spring rates: no published figures were found.

## Drive modes, launch, exhaust and tech features

### Takeaway
A console dial selects Normal, Weather, Sport, Drag and Track. Drag mode works with launch control. Line lock is fitted. The valved exhaust has four modes: Quiet, Normal, Sport and Track.

### Cited Findings
- The console dial lists Normal, Weather, Sport and "Drag & Track." — [Consumer Guide](https://blog.consumerguide.com/shelby-gt500-gallery/)
- Track mode stiffens the steering, firms the MagneRide dampers and selects the most aggressive transmission program. — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125737_first-drive-review-2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-boasts-drag-car-speed-road-racer-moves)
- Exhaust modes are Quiet, Normal, Sport and Track. — [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=1770890)
- Launch: in Drag mode, holding the brake with the left foot and flooring the throttle engages launch control. Line lock holds the front brakes for a burnout. — [Fox11 / Auto Matters](https://fox11online.com/news/auto-matters/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-ford-delivers-fastest-production-mustang-ever-first-look)

### Gaps
- MyMode and Track Apps contents: not confirmed for the GT500 in any source found.
- Weather-mode specifics: not documented in any source found.

## Paint list with codes, per year

### Takeaway
2020 had 11 colors. Rapid Red and Twister Orange were extra-cost. 2021 added Antimatter Blue, Carbonized Gray and Grabber Yellow, and dropped Grabber Lime, Kona Blue, Magnetic and Wimbledon White (see round 1 notes). 2022 added Code Orange (CN) and reportedly Eruption Green. The prompt's guesses of "Grabber Lime PP" and "Twister Orange KU" are wrong: the codes are F9 and CA.

### Cited Findings

  - Twister Orange Tri-Coat CA (extra cost)
  - Rapid Red Metallic Tinted Clearcoat D4 (extra cost)
  - Velocity Blue E7
  - Grabber Lime F9
  - Ford Performance Blue FM
  - Shadow Black G1
  - Magnetic J7
  - Iconic Silver JS
  - Kona Blue L6
  - Race Red PQ
  - Oxford White YZ
  - Extra-cost prices reportedly $495 and $395 (which color carries which price is ambiguous in the snippet).
  — [Ford Authority 2020 GT500 colors](https://fordauthority.com/2019/09/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-colors/)
- Manufacturer codes: Twister Orange M7434, Grabber Lime M7435. — [mustangattitude 2020](https://mustangattitude.com/mustang/2020shelby.shtml) (search snippet)
- 2021 additions and codes: Grabber Yellow CB (M7451), Antimatter Blue HX (M7456), Carbonized Gray M7 (M7458). Performance Blue FM (M7412) is GT500-exclusive. — [mustangattitude 2021](https://www.mustangattitude.com/mustang/2021shelby.shtml) (snippet; sourced from paintref)
- 2022: Code Orange CN (M7468), a Ford Performance-exclusive color. — [mustangattitude 2022](https://www.mustangattitude.com/mustang/2022shelby.shtml) (snippet)
- 2022: a headline says the GT500 "gains new Eruption Green color" (page blocked). — [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2021/12/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-gains-new-eruption-green-color-first-look/)
- 2022 Heritage Edition: Brittany Blue with Wimbledon White stripes, $2,140 with vinyl stripes or $12,140 with painted stripes. — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1134161_2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-gets-retro-look)

## Production numbers

### Takeaway
No official Ford breakdown exists. A forum compilation puts 2020 at 5,860 cars. Grabber Lime (376) and Race Red (298) were the rarest colors in that compilation.

### Cited Findings
- 2020 compilation:
  - Total: 5,860.
  - By color: "Fast Pitch Blue" 843 (likely Ford Performance Blue), Shadow Black 829, Rapid Red 775, Oxford White 737, Grabber Lime 376, Race Red 298.
  - Forum users say Ford has not released detailed breakdowns.
  — [mustang6g post](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3548209); [build stats thread](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/build-statistics-for-2020-2022-run.179732/latest)
- A dealer cites the same 5,860 total and calls Grabber Lime the rarest 2020 color. — [Legendary Motorcar](https://legendarymotorcar.com/inventory/2020-shelby-gt500-2955)
- A Barrett-Jackson listing claims "one of 5,000." This conflicts with the forum total and looks unreliable. — [Barrett-Jackson](https://barrett-jackson.com/Events/Event/Details/2020-FORD-SHELBY-GT500-244661)

### Gaps
- Not found: 2021 and 2022 totals, package-level counts (CFTP, Handling, Tech) and the Heritage Edition count (round 1's 225 vs 255 conflict is unresolved). mustangattitude, which likely holds these figures, was blocked.

## Lap times

### Takeaway
- MotorTrend, Laguna Seca: 1:33.84 with Randy Pobst in a CFTP car (Best Driver's Car 2020).
- Car and Driver Lightning Lap 2021, VIR Grand Course: 2:44.6. This comes from a repost and the driver was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Laguna Seca: 1:33.84 (FastestLaps lists 1:33.810), Randy Pobst, CFTP car with Cup 2 tires. That is quicker than the Camaro ZL1 1LE's 1:34.30. — [musclecarsandtrucks](https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/randy-pobst-blasts-the-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-around-laguna-seca/); [FastestLaps](https://fastestlaps.com/tests/psfo0zn3hom5); [Ford Authority, Jan 2021](https://fordauthority.com/2021/01/watch-the-mustang-shelby-gt500-hot-lap-laguna-seca-video/)
- VIR Lightning Lap 2021: 2:44.6 for the 2020 GT500. — [Chicago Garage repost](https://www.thechicagogarage.com/posts/4217307); [Henry Payne](https://henrypayne.com/index.php/2021/02/payne-on-lightning-lap-2021-corvette-mustang-gt500-porsche-ev-shine)

### Gaps
- Not found: Lightning Lap driver and spec (likely CFTP), and any Nürburgring, Buttonwillow or Road America times. Ford reportedly ran Nürburgring testing, but no published time was found.
