# Media Catalog: 2020-2022 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500

Verification method: I confirmed YouTube IDs and channel names on 2026-10-16 through the YouTube oEmbed endpoint (author_name). Other URLs came from web search and fetch results. Anything I could not verify is listed under Gaps.

## Official Ford press imagery (media.ford.com, Ford Performance, reveal, Heritage Edition, Code Red) and Ford usage terms

### Takeaway
Ford Media Center has an official 2022 Mustang product page that covers the GT500 and the Heritage Edition. The reveal imagery from the 2019 Detroit show (NAIAS) appears mostly as Ford-credited galleries on third-party sites. I found no published blanket reuse licence from Ford. Treat Ford press images as editorial-only unless Ford grants permission in writing. Code Red is a Shelby American product, not a Ford product, so its media comes from Shelby American.

### Cited Findings
- The Ford Media Center 2022 Mustang page describes the "Throwback Tribute: Mustang Shelby GT500 Heritage Edition", which has Wimbledon White over-the-top stripes. URL (returned a 301 redirect when checked): https://media.ford.com/content/fordmedia/fna/us/en/products/icons/mustang/2022-mustang.html — [Ford Media Center](https://media.ford.com/content/fordmedia/fna/us/en/products/icons/mustang/2022-mustang.html)
- The Heritage Edition was a limited run in Brittany Blue. Dealer orders opened on Nov 15, 2021 — [DBusiness](https://www.dbusiness.com/daily-news/ford-debuts-2022-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition/); [Ford Authority](https://fordauthority.com/2021/11/2022-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-heritage-edition-officially-revealed/amp)
- Pricing: base $72,900; Heritage Edition +$2,140 for painted stripes or +$12,140 for hand-painted stripes — [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/?p=2341964)
- Auto123 hosts a 113-photo gallery of the 2020 GT500 credited to Ford. It is useful as a mirror for finding shots, but it is not a licensing source — [Auto123 gallery](https://www.auto123.com/en/multimedia/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-pictures/120317)
- Photos of the reveal at the 2019 Detroit show — [Auto123 Detroit 2019](https://www.auto123.com/en/news/amp/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-debuts-detroit/65485/); [Ford Authority real-world photos](https://fordauthority.com/2019/02/2020-ford-mustang-shelby-gt500-first-real-world-photos)

- The terms for Ford's From the Road site say "Ford grants no license to its or any other property owner's intellectual property through the use of this site" — [Ford terms](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/terms-and-conditions.html)
- The Ford Archives (fordimages.com) license historical images for a fee. Its listed prices: $250 per still for publications, and social media marketing from $100 per image per post. Editorial use is limited to credentialed media — [fordimages copyright licensing](https://fordimages.archivea.com/pages/copyright-licensing)

### Inferences
- Media-site press photos are released for news and editorial coverage. If an app uses them for product UI or marketing, the team should get written permission from Ford Communications.
- For Code Red imagery and video, contact Shelby American directly (it runs the Shelby American YouTube channel listed below).

### Gaps

## Audio sources and licensing

### Takeaway
I found no openly licensed recording of a GT500 specifically. Options: (a) commission your own recording; (b) license a recording from an SFX vendor; (c) contact a YouTuber to license their audio. Do not rip audio from YouTube, because that breaches YouTube's Terms of Service and the creator's copyright.

### Cited Findings
- Web search found no Freesound entry for a GT500 exhaust recording. Results were limited to aftermarket exhaust and forum content — [Ford Authority Borla](https://fordauthority.com/2020/07/new-borla-atak-2020-shelby-gt500-exhaust-delivers-aggressive-sound-video/)
- The Freesound API requires authentication (it returned HTTP 401 without a key). Each Freesound sound carries its own CC0, CC-BY or CC-BY-NC licence — [freesound.org](https://freesound.org/apiv2/search/text/?query=mustang)

### Inferences
- If Freesound has a "Mustang V8" or "supercharged V8" clip under CC0 or CC-BY, it can serve as a generic stand-in. It must not be labelled as a GT500.

### Gaps
- I did not verify specific GT500 catalogue items at SFX vendors (Pro Sound Effects, Soundsnap, BOOM Library).

## Licensing guidance: YouTube embedding vs hosting, and Ford/Shelby/Cobra trademarks

### Takeaway
Embed YouTube videos with the official player or IFrame API. This is allowed under YouTube's API Terms, while downloading and re-hosting needs a licence from the creator. The words "Shelby" and "GT500" and the Cobra logo are trademarks. The Cobra mark is owned by Ford and licensed to Carroll Shelby Licensing. Commercial use of the logos needs licences.

### Cited Findings
- A 2008 legal summary states Shelby is the "exclusive worldwide licensee of the COBRA trademark and logo, which are owned by Ford Motor Company" — [IP trademark attorney](https://www.iptrademarkattorney.com/los-angeles-trademark-shelby-car-racing-motorsports-automobiles-lawyer/)
- Carroll Shelby Licensing (founded 1988) holds Carroll Shelby's trademarks and vehicle design rights. It runs a licensing program whose licensees include Mattel, Sony and EA — [The Auto Channel 2001](https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2001/05/15/020888.html)
- Shelby has enforced these rights against replica makers, for example in the settlement with Superformance — [Superformance](https://superformance.com/news-article/carroll-shelby-and-superformance-intl-settle-infringement-dispute); [Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/carroll-shelby-and-superperformance-settle-trademark-de-120327)
- Ford's own marketing renders the name as "Shelby® GT500®" (see the title of the official reveal video) — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UzruyIE3_g0)

### Inferences
- Plain-text nominative use (for example "2020 Ford Mustang Shelby GT500 specs") is generally acceptable. Do not use logos or the Cobra emblem, and do not imply endorsement, without licences from Ford Brand Licensing and Carroll Shelby Licensing. This is general guidance, not legal advice; have counsel confirm it.

### Gaps
- The trademark and licensing sources date from 2001-2008. I did not verify the current status of the Ford/Shelby agreement or current licensing contacts.
