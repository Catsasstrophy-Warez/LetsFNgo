## Found
- **Ford TSB 24-2327** (high), dated Oct 22, 2024, for 2020–2022 Mustang Shelby GT500. It covers a transmission fluid leak where the rear cooler tubes meet the transmission case. The fix is to file the dimple flat and fit new rear cooler tubes (KR3Z-7R081-C) in the correct order. This is the first Ford document backing the vendor claim about DCT cooler lines that is already in recalls.ts.
